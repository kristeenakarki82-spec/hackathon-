# Team Pomu — AI Study Companion

A hackathon-winning learning platform that turns confusion into confidence.

## Product positioning statement
Team Pomu is the first student-centered AI tutoring experience that adapts to grade level, learning goal, time availability, energy, preferred style, and struggle areas in a single session.

## Tagline options
- Study smarter, not harder
- Confidence-built AI tutoring
- Adaptive learning for every student
- Personalized study in seconds

## Mission statement
Our mission is to make learning feel less stressful and more effective by delivering instantly personalized study sessions that match each student’s energy, goal, and progress.

## Elevator pitch
Team Pomu is an AI Study Companion designed for students who need personalized support right now. It captures grade level, goal, time, energy, style, and struggle areas, then instantly delivers adaptive explanations, quizzes, visual summaries, and study plans. The platform adjusts difficulty in real time, detects confusion before it stalls progress, and tracks performance patterns to guide the next best step.

## What it does

- Creates a tailored study profile with grade, goal, energy, time, style, and struggle areas.
- Generates adaptive explanations, mnemonic flashcards, and exam-style quizzes instantly.
- Includes smart study roadmaps and real-time difficulty shifting.
- Provides demo-ready UI and a student dashboard for progress tracking.

## Key features

- Real-time difficulty adjustment
- Confusion detection and follow-up simplification
- Energy-aware explanation style
- Memory retention booster mode
- Mnemonic + flashcard generator
- Adaptive exam simulation mode
- Weakness pattern detection
- Personalized study roadmap
- Teacher/parent dashboard support

## How to use

1. Open `index.html` in a web browser.
2. Fill out the smart profile form.
3. Click "Start my smart study" and explore the live demo.
